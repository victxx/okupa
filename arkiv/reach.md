# Reach

- **Who it is for:**
  - People who like a small companion that lives on a real device (here, a 2009 Nintendo DSi).
  - Their friends without a DSi, who join from a browser.
- **Would they use it:** the gift is the social act. A drawing for a friend's monkey costs about one cent on Ethereum today, so you send few, with intention.
- **Can they try it today:** yes, in a browser, with [`web/README.md`](../web/README.md). The console needs a DSi with a homebrew loader.
- **After Mumbai:**
  - Gifts from the DSi to friends.
  - The DSi reading its mailbox. Arkiv's RPC speaks TLS 1.2, which the console already uses for 0G.
  - Then 3D gifts.
