A little okupa that lives on your Nintendo DSi.

It joins Ethereum by itself, peer to peer, with no server in between. Friends anywhere, on another DSi or in a browser, can give it pictures.

Play it on a DSi

  1. Downloadokupa.nds and the guide below.
  2. Copyokupa.nds to the SD card, for example /roms/nds/.
  3. Startit from your homebrew menu. It makes sd:/okupa/ by itself.
